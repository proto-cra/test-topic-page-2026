At the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), we believe you should have support at your fingertips—without waiting in line. That’s why we’ve built secure digital services that empower you to find answers to most questions in minutes.

On this page is essential information on better ways to find the answers you need without calling, as well as what to expect when contacting the CRA, if you do need to call.

Find answers to common questions regarding benefit and credit payments

Did you know that many of the reasons people call the CRA can be resolved quickly and conveniently using our self-serve digital options?

To serve you better, we’ll be closely monitoring the top benefit and credit call drivers and updating this section periodically. By highlighting the most common reasons people call and showcasing how to resolve them online, we aim to help you skip the line and to quickly get the answers you need.

Don’t forget that you need to file your 2025 tax return to get, or continue to get, the payments you’re entitled to. If you haven’t filed yet, it’s not too late!

Below are three of the top reasons for calls and how you can save time by resolving them online:

How to determine what payments you will receive

The CRA will issue you an electronic Notice of Determination for each applicable benefit and credit you are entitled to receive. You can find these important documents by signing in to your CRA account.

Your Notice(s) of Determination will tell you:

  • Which benefits and credits you qualify for
  • How much you’ll receive from July 2026 to June 2027
How to find out when payments are issued

Find out when your benefit and credit payments will be issued by visiting Benefits payment dates. You can also sign up for our electronic mailing list to receive email reminders about one week before we issue the payment.

How to report income earned outside of Canada online

If you have already registered for a CRA account, you no longer have to call us to report the income you, or your non resident spouse, earned outside of Canada. 

As of May 2026, the fastest method to provide a statement of income is online using your CRA account. Your statement of income is updated immediately after you submit the change, with no wait time.

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select your Individual account to access My Account
  3. Select “Benefits and credits”
  4. Go to “Statement of Income (SOI)”
  5. Update your information

If you have not already registered for a CRA account, your can mail the information to your tax centre. 

Find more information regarding these programs, including eligibility requirements, by visiting canada.ca/cra-benefits  or refer to:

  • Guide RC4210 for Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (formerly known as the GST/HST credit)
  • Guide T4114 for Canada child benefit

Use digital services first

Many tasks can be resolved without speaking to a service representative.

Use our digital services for individuals when:

  • You want to register for a CRA account
  • You want to confirm a payment or balance owing
  • You want to update your personal information
  • You want to calculate your TFSA contribution room information
  • You want to know the status of your tax return or other CRA processing times
  • You need to change your tax return
  • You have a general question about credits and benefits for individuals
  • You need a proof of income statement, notice of assessment, or a copy of your tax slips
  • You suspect a scam, fraud, or identity theft
  • You want to verify if it’s the CRA calling you
  • You represent someone who died
Use our digital services for businesses when :
  • You want to register for a business number and CRA program accounts
  • You want to find an existing business number
  • You want to submit an enquiry for a business
  • You want to update your business information
  • You want to access and view your business correspondence
  • You want to sign up for email notification from the CRA - Business

Here are the digital services available to help you with these tasks:

GenAI chatbot

The GenAI chatbot is available 24/7 to answer questions relating to CRA programs and services, such as how to access a CRA account.

Prompts you can ask the GenAI chatbot
"How do I register for a CRA account?"
The chatbot can walk users through the registration process, explain what documents are needed, how to get a CRA security code, and what to expect during setup.
"How do I change my address or direct deposit information?"
The chatbot can walk users through securely updating their information through My Account, ensuring uninterrupted benefit and tax-related payments.
"How do I check the status of my tax refund?"
The chatbot can guide the user to sign-in to their CRA account and find the refund status, including expected payment dates and any issues.
"Do I qualify for the GST/HST credit or the Canada Child Benefit?"
The chatbot can explain eligibility criteria, how to apply or ensure you're registered, and how to check payment schedules and amounts.
"When will I receive my next benefit or payment from the CRA?"
The chatbot can help users check expected payment dates for programs like the GST/HST credit, Canada Child Benefit, or tax refunds, and guide them to My Account for full details and history.

Online chat in My Account

The CRA offers an online chat service within My Account that lets you discuss account-specific issues with a live CRA service representative.

This chat service is available for both account-specific and general questions about:
  • Personal Income Tax
  • Benefits
  • My Account
  • Ordering CRA forms and publications
  • Payments to the CRA
  • Savings and Pension Plans
  • Instalments

How to use the chat service

  1. Sign in to your CRA account and go to My Account
  2. Select the chat widget at the bottom right corner of the page
  3. Accept the privacy statement and select your topic to begin

The online chat service is available in My Account Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm ET.

Know before you call

If your situation can’t be resolved online, you may need to call us. For example:

  • You're locked out of your CRA account

    Regain access to your CRA account without having to call

    Users who lost access or have been locked out of their CRA account because they have forgotten their existing credentials can now register again for a new credential and regain access to their account. Learn more, Register for a CRA account.

  • You’re dealing with a complex reassessment or an audit notice
  • You need help with your CRA debt

Here’s how to prepare before calling:

  • Check the wait times to contact the CRA
  • Have everything you need on hand
  • Find the right phone number

When average wait times reach 30 minutes, we redirect incoming calls to our automated phone services. This helps us serve as many people as possible, as fairly as possible.

The automated phone service line can help you:
  • Check the status of your tax return
  • Get an estimate on your refund amount
  • Find out your CRA account balance
  • Check your contribution amounts (TFSA and RRSP)
  • Get a proof of income statement
  • Request a remittance voucher
  • Check benefit and credit eligibility, payment dates, and amounts (Canada Child Benefit and GST/HST credit)
  • Request a form
  • Set up a payment arrangement for personal income taxes
  • Apply for the Disability Tax Credit
  • File a GST/HST return
  • Report suspected fraud, identity theft, or suspicious activity on your CRA account

Tip: If the wait time is showing "Not available", it means:

  • calls are being redirected to automated services (due to high volumes);
  • contact centres are closed; or
  • the CRA is experiencing a phone outage.

Call only when a wait time is displayed.

Facts behind the service

We recognize there is an opportunity for us to enhance our service delivery to better meet your needs and expectations. We’re taking action to make improvements in the very short term.

 
 

Behind each of those calls is a person with an important question: a parent trying to understand a child benefit, a retiree looking for their refund, or a newcomer navigating our tax system for the first time. And on the other side of the line, there are CRA service representatives who do their best to answer your questions.

We continue to improve our self-serve options so you can get the help you need, faster and on your own time — without waiting on hold.

Myth-busting

Myth: “The CRA does not answer the phone.” 

Fact: We understand how frustrating it can be to wait for help. The CRA answers between 36,000 and 38,000 calls every day to support Canadians with their needs. When wait times go beyond an average of 30 minutes, we redirect calls to automated services to provide you with secure, easy-to-use options.

Myth: “Letting more people join the phone queue would mean more calls get answered.” 

Fact: Call volumes currently exceed our capacity to respond. When we reach full capacity, we redirect calls to automated services. Think of it like a full glass of water: adding more doesn’t help, it just overflows. Letting more callers into the queue wouldn’t make it possible to answer more calls, it would only increase wait time and frustration.

Myth: “Calling the CRA is the best way for me to make changes to my personal information.” 

Fact: The fastest, most convenient way to update your personal information is through your CRA account—there is no need to call. Keeping your personal information up to date ensures you receive all correspondence and payments without delay.

Myth: “The CRA does not provide accurate information to callers about individual taxes, benefits, and business taxes.” 

Fact: The CRA handles more than 30 million calls annually, 80 percent are related to individual accounts. The Quality Monitoring Program reviews 100,000+ calls yearly, with the latest findings showing we have results of over 90 percent for professional and efficient service, as well as for accurate information provided. We are working to address the accuracy issues raised by the Auditor General for questions of a general nature, which make up 20 percent of our calls.
